1. Who we are

Datamart Warmup is run by Datamart Inc., a California corporation ("Datamart", "we", "us"). For the data about you and your account, we are the controller. For the data about the people you email (your contacts and recipients), you are the controller and we process it on your behalf, only to run the service for you.

2. What this policy covers

This policy covers warmup.dtm.global: the website, the app, the admin and API services behind it, and the emails it sends. It does not cover services you connect to it, such as your email provider or CRM, which have their own policies.

3. Data we collect

Account data

Your name, email address, password (stored only as a one-way hash), company and workspace name, your role, and your two-step sign-in or passkey settings.

Mailbox data

For each mailbox you connect: its address, server settings and the password or app password needed to connect, plus sync state and health data such as bounces and inbox placement.

Message data

Warmup messages your mailboxes send and receive, campaign messages and replies, the recipients and contacts you import or create, and campaign events such as opens, clicks, replies, bounces and unsubscribes.

Files

Attachments, imports, exports, profile pictures and logos you upload.

Usage and security data

Server logs, IP addresses, browser type, sign-in events and an audit log of important actions in your workspace. We use these to run, secure and fix the service.

We do not use third-party analytics, advertising trackers or session recording on the website or in the app.

4. How we use it

  • to provide the service: connecting mailboxes, running warmup, sending your campaigns, syncing your inbox and showing deliverability results;
  • to keep the warmup network healthy: pairing mailboxes across workspaces, and spotting and stopping abuse;
  • to send you service email, such as sign-in links, invitations, alerts and important notices;
  • to secure the service, investigate problems and enforce our Terms of Service;
  • to meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers or use it for advertising.

5. Mailbox data

We read and act on a connected mailbox only to provide the features you turn on: finding and handling warmup messages, syncing replies and inbox messages you choose to see in the app, and sending the mail you or your campaigns send. People at Datamart do not read your messages unless you ask us to for support, or it is needed for security or required by law.

Warmup messages that reach your mailbox are generated by the service and are deleted after a retention period, 30 days unless you choose a different period. When you disconnect a mailbox, we delete its stored password and stop all access to it.

6. AI features

Molty and the AI features in campaigns run on Anthropic's Claude models through Amazon Bedrock, inside our AWS account. When you use them, we send the model only what the request needs, such as your question and the relevant workspace data. Amazon Bedrock does not share this data with the model provider and does not use it to train models. We do not use your data to train AI models either.

7. Legal bases

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on: performing our contract with you (running the service); our legitimate interests (securing the service, preventing abuse and keeping the warmup network healthy); your consent, where we ask for it; and legal obligations.

8. How we protect it

  • All traffic to the service is encrypted with TLS.
  • Mailbox passwords and access tokens are encrypted before they are stored, with keys kept separately from the database.
  • Uploaded files are kept in a private, encrypted Amazon S3 bucket that cannot be reached from the internet directly.
  • The servers run in Amazon Web Services in the United States, reachable only through the web ports the service needs.
  • Accounts can be protected with two-step sign-in or a passkey.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

9. Who we share it with

We use these service providers, who process data only on our instructions:

  • Amazon Web Services (United States): servers, database, file storage (S3), platform email (Amazon SES) and AI models (Amazon Bedrock).
  • Cloudflare: domain name (DNS) service. Your traffic does not pass through Cloudflare.

Your connected email providers (such as Google or Microsoft) and any tools you connect receive data as needed to do what you ask, under their own policies. Other members of the warmup network receive the warmup messages your mailboxes send them, which include your mailbox's address and display name, but never your inbox, contacts or campaigns.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it, to protect people's safety or our rights, or as part of a merger or sale of the business, in which case this policy continues to apply to it.

10. How long we keep it

  • Account and workspace data: while your account or workspace exists. When you delete a workspace, its mailboxes, messages, contacts, campaigns and files are deleted.
  • Warmup messages: deleted from mailboxes after 30 days by default.
  • Campaign events such as opens and clicks: up to 365 days.
  • Audit log: 90 days.
  • Backups: nightly database backups and deleted files are kept for 30 days, then removed for good.
  • Suppression and unsubscribe records: as long as needed to keep honouring them.

11. Cookies

We use only the cookies and browser storage the service needs to work: keeping you signed in, protecting sign-in, and remembering settings such as your current workspace. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

Campaign emails can include open and click tracking, which you can turn off per campaign. If you send campaigns, you are responsible for telling your recipients about it where the law requires.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to withdraw consent. California residents have the rights the CCPA gives them, and we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context advertising. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

You can change or delete most of your data yourself in the app. For anything else, contact us (see below). If you are a contact or recipient of one of our members, please contact that member first, as they control your data; we will help them respond.

13. International transfers

The service runs in the United States. If you use it from elsewhere, your data is transferred to and stored in the United States. Where the law requires, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

14. Children

The service is for businesses and is not meant for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data about children.

15. Changes

We may update this policy. If a change is significant, we will tell you by email or in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

16. Contact

Questions or requests about your data? Contact the Datamart team through dtm.global.
