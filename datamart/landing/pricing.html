Pricing FAQ

The small print, in plain words.

Do I need a card to sign up?

No. You sign up, create your workspace and connect your mailboxes. There is nothing to pay and no card on file.

What are AI credits used for?

Anything the AI does for you: answering and acting in Molty, and writing AI lines in your campaigns. Warmup, sending, the inbox and the dashboards use no credits at all.

What happens when I run out of free credits?

Only the AI pauses until the credits reset at midnight UTC. Warmup and everything else keeps running. If you need more on the same day, you can unlock extra credits for your workspace.

Will warmup ever become a paid plan?

Warmup gets better with every member who joins, so it is free for the community. Datamart pays for it. The only thing that costs money is extra AI, because every AI answer has a real cost.
