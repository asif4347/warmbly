How it works

Three steps, then it runs on its own.

Warmup works best when mail goes back and forth between many different companies. That is exactly what a community of agencies has.

Step 1

Create your workspace

Each company signs up for its own workspace. Invite your team into it. Other companies never see inside it.

Step 2

Connect your mailboxes

Google Workspace and Gmail, Microsoft 365 and Outlook, or any mailbox with SMTP and IMAP. Passwords are stored encrypted.

Step 3

Warm up with the network

Your mailboxes send to and reply with other members' mailboxes, never with each other. Volume ramps up slowly, day by day.

Why it's free

The members are the network.

Paid warmup tools charge for access to their pool of mailboxes. Here, the pool is us: every mailbox a member connects makes warmup better for everyone else.

Datamart pays for the servers and running costs, because better deliverability helps every agency in the community, ours included.

Join the network

One workspace per company. That is what stops a company's mailboxes from warming each other.

Only warmup mail moves between members. Your real inbox, contacts and campaigns stay in your workspace.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is it really free?

Yes. No card, no plan and no cap on mailboxes. Datamart covers the servers and running costs.

What do I need to connect a mailbox?

SMTP and IMAP access to the mailbox, plus SPF, DKIM and DMARC records on its domain. For Gmail and Google Workspace that means an app password. For Microsoft 365, SMTP sending has to be switched on for the mailbox.

Can other members read my email?

No. Members only ever receive the warmup messages your mailboxes send them. Nobody outside your workspace can see your inbox, your contacts or your campaigns.

Will my own mailboxes warm each other?

No. Mail within one company looks nothing like real outreach, so your mailboxes are always paired with other members' mailboxes. That is why each company needs its own workspace.

How long should I warm a new mailbox?

Give a new mailbox at least two weeks before it sends cold email, and keep warmup running at a low level after that. The health score tells you when it is in good shape.

Who can join?

Agencies and teams in the Datamart community, and the people they bring along. Sign up for your company's workspace and connect your mailboxes.
